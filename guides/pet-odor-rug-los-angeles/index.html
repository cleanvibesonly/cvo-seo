7 Ways to Get Pet Odor Out of a Rug in Los Angeles

Pet odor in a rug is rarely just on the surface. It soaks into the pile, the backing, and sometimes the pad underneath, which is why the smell keeps coming back after a quick clean. Here are seven ways to deal with it, roughly in order of how stubborn the odor is.

1. Professional hot-water extraction (the reset button)

When the smell returns no matter what you try, the odor has reached the base of the rug where home methods can't get to it. 2. Blot fresh accidents right away

For a fresh accident, speed matters more than anything. Blot with a clean cloth and cold water, pressing down instead of rubbing so you lift the liquid out rather than grinding it deeper. Keep swapping to a dry section of cloth until little transfers.

3. Baking soda overnight

Once the spot is dry, sprinkle baking soda generously over the area, work it lightly into the pile, and leave it overnight. It's a mild, rug-safe deodorizer that pulls in odor. Vacuum thoroughly the next morning.

4. White vinegar solution

Mix one part white vinegar to two parts warm water, mist the area lightly (don't soak it), and blot dry. The vinegar smell fades as it dries and takes a lot of the odor with it. Spot-test a hidden corner first on colored or wool rugs.

5. A real enzyme cleaner

For set-in pet smells, a pet-specific enzyme cleaner from any pet store is the DIY workhorse. Enzymes digest the odor-causing proteins rather than covering them. Follow the label dwell time, and keep pets off the rug until it's fully dry.

6. Air it out in the LA sun

We have the climate for it. Dry air and UV help neutralize odor, so hang smaller rugs outside for a few hours on a dry day. Keep vivid or wool rugs out of prolonged direct sun to avoid fading.

7. When the rug is past saving, donate or dispose responsibly

If the odor has soaked into the backing or pad and the rug isn't worth restoring, get rid of it the right way. In the City of LA, schedule a free Bulky-Item Pickup through MyLA311. In Pasadena, the city runs its own bulky-item collection. If the rug is still clean and usable, Goodwill Southern California accepts donations. Don't leave a rug curbside without scheduling a pickup, since that can mean a fine.

One note on antique and hand-knotted rugs

If you have a genuine hand-knotted wool or silk rug, don't DIY it and don't steam it.
